# User guide

## Getting started

1. Open VidTube and choose **Sign in**, then **Create an account**.
2. Pick a username, enter your email and a password with at least 8 characters, a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter and a number.
3. Check your inbox for the verification email and open the link. You can resend it from the banner at the top of the page.

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link.

## Watching

- **Home** shows the latest videos. Use the category chips, or the Categories list in the sidebar, to filter.
- **Trending** shows what is popular right now. **Shorts** is a vertical feed that plays as you scroll.
- Open a video to watch it. Below the player you can **like or dislike**, **Share** the link, **Save** it to a playlist and read or write comments. **Show more** expands the description.
- Use the search bar, or the microphone button in supported browsers, to search.

## Your library

- **Subscriptions** lists new videos from channels you follow. The sidebar also lists the channels themselves.
- **History** shows what you watched. You can remove entries, clear everything or pause history.
- **Liked videos** lists videos you liked.
- **Library** holds your playlists, including Watch later. Create playlists, rename them and remove videos.

## Comments

Write a comment under any video. Reply to a comment from its **Reply** link. You can edit or delete your own comments, and video owners can delete comments on their videos.

## Subscriptions and notifications

Press **Subscribe** on a video or channel page. The bell in the header shows new videos, new subscribers, comments, replies and likes in real time. Open the Notifications page to mark them read.

## Creating a channel

Choose **Create channel** from your profile, pick a name and a handle, then upload videos with the upload button in the header. Add a title, description, tags, category and visibility:

- **Public** is visible to everyone.
- **Unlisted** is visible to people with the link.
- **Private** is visible only to you.

Mark a video as a **Short** to show it in the Shorts feed. A new upload shows as processing for a few seconds before it goes live.

## Studio

Open **Studio** from the sidebar or the menu next to the bell. It shows views, watch time, subscriber growth and your top videos for the last 7, 28, 90 or 365 days, or all time.

## Settings

From **Settings** you can change your name and username, profile photo and cover image, change your password, switch between light and dark themes and delete your account.
